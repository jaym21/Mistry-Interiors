04 Services

What we deliver.

Turnkey Office Fit-Out

Complete interior execution from bare shell to move-in ready. Civil, MEP, finishes, furniture — one contractor, one responsibility, one standard.

Corporate Interiors

Premium workspace environments for MNCs, tech companies, and established corporations. Designed for performance, built for people.

MEP Works

Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems executed with engineering precision. The infrastructure that makes spaces actually work.

Furniture & Procurement

Sourcing, procurement, and installation of furniture systems. From workstations to executive suites — quality controlled, cost optimized.

Industrial & Site Offices

Structural and interior works for industrial plants and site offices. Proven delivery for JSW, Tata Steel, AMNS, and India's largest manufacturers.

Project Management

End-to-end coordination with architects, designers, and vendors. We manage complexity so you don't have to.

05 Process

How we work.

Step 01

Consultation & Scope

We study the drawings, visit the site, understand the brief. Every project begins with listening — not selling.

Step 02

Planning & Estimation

Detailed BOQ preparation, material specification, timeline mapping. Transparent pricing — no hidden costs, no surprises at settlement.

Step 03

Execution & Quality Control

On-site supervision, daily progress tracking, quality checkpoints at every stage. We manage the chaos so architects and designers don't have to.

Handover & Beyond

Snag-free handover, complete documentation, post-completion support. The relationship doesn't end at handover — it begins there.
